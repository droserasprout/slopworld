# Audio scheduling dependencies

CPAL 0.17.3 is MIT licensed. Its Linux real-time scheduling support uses
audio_thread_priority 0.34.0 (MPL-2.0), dbus 0.6.5 and libdbus-sys 0.2.7
(MIT selected from Apache-2.0 OR MIT). Full texts are in the versioned directories.

The MPL-covered audio_thread_priority source is used unmodified and is available
at https://crates.io/api/v1/crates/audio_thread_priority/0.34.0/download.
Its file-level license does not change SlopWorld's MIT license.
