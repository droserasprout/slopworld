# Colony life and music

## The colony

Working agents build paving, graves, monuments, and ancient buildings.
Finished work spreads plague outward from completed sites. Fire cannot spread
ahead of the plague front. In normal mode, fully plagued ground suppresses
wild-plant regrowth.

Grandma mode replaces plague damage with flowers and permits wild regrowth.
Fire containment remains active.

## Arrivals

The opening persona core drops as a skyfaller. Later agents arrive in drop pods
with a short opening delay.

## Jukebox

Open the map jukebox or status-bar jukebox control to play music. The OST is built
in; native Linux playback also supports radio and experimental Spotify playback.
Sidecar mode plays the bundled OST through RimWorld and offers mute and Settings.
See the [Jukebox guide](../guides/jukebox.md) for source setup and controls.
